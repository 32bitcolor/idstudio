# Getting started

> Tavoro is the workspace that runs your whole instructional-design process in one place — intake, planning, design, SME review, and delivery — sitting above the authoring tools and LMS you already use. This guide gets you oriented in a couple of minutes.

## Your workspace

Everything in Tavoro lives inside a **workspace** — your team’s shared home for boards, projects, storyboards, and whiteboards. Everyone you invite becomes a member of the workspace, with one of three roles:

- **Admin** — manages members, groups, access, and workspace settings, in addition to doing the regular work.
- **Manager** — an oversight role that sees everything across the workspace like an admin, but without the member/group/settings controls.
- **Member** — does the day-to-day work: boards, projects, storyboards, reviews.

## Finding your way around

The app has one consistent shell, so every screen feels the same:

- The **left sidebar** lists your modules — My Work, Intake, Boards, Projects, Sprints, Storyboards, and Whiteboards, plus Team for admins and managers — with Help and your account at the bottom. Collapse it with the toggle in the header when you want more room.
- The **breadcrumbs** at the top show where you are and let you jump back up a level.
- The **Dashboard** (the Tavoro logo, top-left) is your at-a-glance home: active projects, what’s awaiting your review, and upcoming milestones.
- The header’s **Search** box (or **⌘K** on a Mac, **Ctrl K** elsewhere) jumps to any board, project, storyboard, or card, and **New** creates a board, project, storyboard, or whiteboard from anywhere.

## A good first run

1. Open **Settings → Account** and change your password from whatever you were given. (If your workspace uses single sign-on, you sign in with your work account instead and can skip this.)
2. If you’re an admin, add your team in **Settings → Members** (or connect your identity provider in [Settings → Single sign-on](sso.md)), and organize them in **Settings → Groups**.
3. Create your first **Project**, pick a methodology ([ADDIE or SAM](addie-sam.md)), and let Tavoro seed the phases.
4. Spin up a **Board** for your production pipeline and start turning the plan into tracked, assignable work.

## Make it yours

Head to **Settings → Account → Appearance** to switch between 17 built-in themes — from clean Light and GitHub to Dracula, Rosé Pine, Everforest, and more. Your choice is remembered on this device and applies instantly across the whole app.

> **Tip.** New to instructional-design process? Start with the [ADDIE & SAM explainer](addie-sam.md) — it’s the backbone of how Projects work here. Then skim the module guides in the sidebar for the features you’ll use most.
